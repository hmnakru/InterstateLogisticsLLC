Our company

More than logistics. A cleaner, safer tomorrow.

Interstate Logistics LLC is a chemical logistics specialist operating across North America. We plan, execute and monitor the movement of bulk liquids, packaged chemicals and regulated materials by truck, rail, intermodal and ocean.

Chemical freight leaves no room for improvisation. Every load is built around the product: the right equipment, the right documentation, a carrier qualified to handle it, and a team that stays with the shipment from pickup to delivery.

Alongside our chemical work we handle standard freight of every description, so one partner covers the whole supply chain.
